Jewelry Manufacturing FAQ

Have questions about wholesale jewelry manufacturing, OEM/ODM customization or sourcing jewelry from China? Explore answers to common questions from jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers working with QIUHE.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are you a direct jewelry manufacturer or a trading company?

QIUHE is a jewelry manufacturer based in Dongguan, Guangdong, China. We work with jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers on jewelry production and OEM/ODM customization. Please contact us for factory verification details.

2. What types of jewelry do you manufacture?

Our product range includes stainless steel jewelry, 925 sterling silver jewelry and fashion jewelry. We manufacture necklaces, earrings, bracelets and rings for wholesale and custom orders. Specific materials and finishes can be discussed according to your project requirements.

3. Do you offer OEM and ODM jewelry manufacturing?

Yes. QIUHE supports OEM/ODM jewelry projects for brands and wholesale customers. You can share product photos, technical drawings or physical samples for an initial manufacturing assessment. Customization options are confirmed based on design requirements and production feasibility.

4. What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Minimum order quantities depend on the jewelry design, material, production process and customization requirements. Please send us your product specifications and estimated quantities so we can confirm the applicable MOQ.

5. How do you ensure jewelry quality?

Quality requirements are reviewed according to the product specifications, materials and finishing requirements of each order. Buyers can discuss inspection criteria, sample approval and any required testing documentation with our team before production.

Sampling, Production & Shipping

6. Can I request jewelry samples before placing a bulk order?

Buyers can contact QIUHE to discuss sample requests before placing a bulk order. Sample availability, development requirements, costs and timelines depend on the selected products and customization needs.

7. What is your jewelry production lead time?

Production lead times vary depending on order quantity, materials, design complexity and manufacturing requirements. Please share your product specifications and target delivery date so our team can assess the production schedule.

8. Can you provide jewelry testing reports for the EU, UK or US markets?

Testing and documentation requirements depend on the jewelry materials, product design and destination market. Please let us know your applicable compliance requirements, such as REACH-related restrictions or nickel release testing. We can review the documentation and testing arrangements required for your specific order.

9. Can you provide custom logo and private label packaging?

QIUHE welcomes inquiries about private label jewelry projects, including logo and packaging requirements. Please share your branding specifications, packaging references and estimated quantities so we can assess the available customization options.

10. Do you support international jewelry shipping?

QIUHE works with overseas jewelry buyers. International shipping options, freight arrangements and applicable trade terms can be discussed according to the destination, order size and shipment requirements.

11. Can I Mix Different Colors Within a 12-Piece MOQ?

Yes. QIUHE allows buyers to mix different colors within the same jewelry design, with a minimum order quantity of 12 pieces. This flexible MOQ helps jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers test different color options while managing inventory and purchasing costs. Please share your preferred color combinations when requesting a quotation.

12. Do You Offer 304 and 316L Stainless Steel Jewelry?

Yes. QIUHE manufactures both 304 and 316L stainless steel jewelry for brands, retailers and wholesale buyers. We produce stainless steel necklaces, earrings, bracelets and rings, with OEM/ODM customization options available. 304 stainless steel is a practical option for many fashion jewelry designs, while 316L stainless steel offers enhanced corrosion resistance, making it a popular choice for higher-specification jewelry collections. Buyers can share their preferred material grade, jewelry designs, surface finishes and estimated order quantities. Our team can review the specifications and recommend suitable manufacturing options for their projects.

13. What Plating Options Are Available for Stainless Steel Jewelry?

QIUHE offers a range of plating and surface finishing options for stainless steel jewelry, including PVD coating, traditional electroplating, polishing and brushed finishes. We manufacture custom stainless steel necklaces, earrings, bracelets and rings for jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers. Available colors and finishes depend on the jewelry design, material grade and production requirements. Buyers can share their preferred plating colors, surface finishes, product designs and estimated order quantities. Our team can review the specifications and recommend suitable finishing options for OEM/ODM stainless steel jewelry projects.

14. Can You Support Jewelry Testing for Nickel Release, Lead and Cadmium?

QIUHE understands the importance of jewelry safety and regulatory compliance for international markets, including the European Union, United Kingdom and United States. We have third-party testing reports available for selected jewelry products and can arrange additional third-party testing based on buyers' specific requirements. Testing may include nickel release, lead content, cadmium content and other applicable requirements, depending on the jewelry materials, product design and destination market. Jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers are welcome to share their target market, testing standards and product specifications. Our team can review available documentation and discuss suitable testing arrangements before bulk production. Please note that testing reports are product-specific, and compliance documentation should be confirmed for each applicable order.

15. How Do You Handle Defective Jewelry in Bulk Orders?

QIUHE takes product quality and customer satisfaction seriously. If a buyer identifies defective jewelry or quality issues in a bulk order, we encourage them to contact our team and provide clear photos or videos showing the reported problems. Our team will review the reported issues against the agreed product specifications and order requirements to help determine the cause and appropriate next steps. Depending on the findings and specific circumstances, QIUHE will discuss suitable solutions with the buyer, which may include replacement products, rework or other mutually agreed arrangements. We value transparent communication and aim to build long-term relationships with jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers through responsible quality issue handling.

16. How Do You Protect Customers' Custom Jewelry Designs?

QIUHE respects the confidentiality and intellectual property of our customers' custom jewelry designs. We understand that original designs, technical drawings, product specifications and development concepts are valuable business assets for jewelry brands and private label buyers. QIUHE can sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) with customers to protect confidential information during OEM/ODM jewelry development and manufacturing. We do not share customers' custom jewelry designs with third parties without their authorization. Jewelry brands, designers and wholesale buyers are welcome to discuss confidentiality requirements with our team before sharing their designs, samples or technical files.

17. How Can Buyers Track Production Progress for Their Jewelry Orders?

QIUHE understands the importance of clear communication and production transparency for international jewelry buyers. We keep in touch with customers through WhatsApp and email to provide updates on their jewelry orders and manufacturing progress. Production photos or videos can also be provided upon request, allowing buyers to better understand the manufacturing status of their products. For OEM/ODM jewelry projects and wholesale orders, buyers are welcome to discuss their preferred communication methods and progress update requirements with our team. Our goal is to maintain effective communication throughout the production process and help jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers manage their sourcing projects with confidence.

QIUHE understands that consistency between approved samples and bulk production is essential for jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers. Before mass production, customers can review and approve jewelry samples to confirm important product details, including materials, dimensions, colors, plating, surface finishes and overall appearance. Once the sample and production specifications are approved, QIUHE can arrange bulk manufacturing according to the agreed requirements. Quality inspections can be carried out during production and before shipment to help identify potential differences and maintain consistency with the approved samples. For OEM/ODM jewelry projects, buyers can also discuss specific inspection criteria, quality standards and approval procedures with our team based on their order requirements. Our goal is to deliver jewelry products that meet the agreed specifications and support reliable, long-term manufacturing partnerships.

19. What Is Your Sample Lead Time and Bulk Production Lead Time?

QIUHE provides jewelry sampling and bulk manufacturing services for jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers. QIUHE provides customized wholesale jewelry quotations for jewelry brands, retailers and wholesale buyers based on their specific product and order requirements.
