Getting started

How to Start Your OEM/ODM Jewelry Project

Share your requirements so we can review the design and discuss an appropriate production approach. The exact development and production stages depend on the product.

  1. Send your concept: reference images, design drawings or physical samples.
  2. Specify your requirements: preferred materials, stones, dimensions, finishes and estimated quantities.
  3. Discuss feasibility: confirm production details and customization needs.
  4. Review a tailored quotation: MOQ and pricing are assessed according to material, design, process and customization requirements.
