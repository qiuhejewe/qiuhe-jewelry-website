Jewelry Sourcing Guide

How to Choose a Reliable Jewelry Manufacturer in China

Finding the right jewelry manufacturing partner is an important decision for brands, retailers and wholesale buyers. Beyond comparing prices, professional buyers should evaluate manufacturing capabilities, material specifications, quality management and communication before placing a bulk order.

1. Verify the Manufacturer's Business and Factory

Start by confirming the supplier's registered business name, operating address and manufacturing activities. Ask whether the company produces jewelry in-house, works with subcontractors or sources finished products from other suppliers.

Factory photographs, production videos, business registration documents and third-party inspections can help buyers evaluate supplier credibility.

2. Evaluate Jewelry Materials and Specifications

Different jewelry materials serve different customer segments and price points. Common options include stainless steel, 925 sterling silver and fashion jewelry.

For stainless steel jewelry, ask about the steel grade, surface finishing and plating process. For sterling silver jewelry, confirm the silver content, plating specifications and any relevant material documentation.

Explore our wholesale stainless steel jewelry and 925 sterling silver jewelry manufacturing pages.

3. Compare Minimum Order Quantities and Pricing

Minimum order quantities can vary depending on the design, material, manufacturing process and level of customization.

When comparing quotations, request clear information about unit pricing, sample costs, tooling charges, packaging costs and applicable order quantities. The lowest quoted unit price may not represent the lowest total purchasing cost.

4. Review OEM and ODM Customization Capabilities

If you are developing a private label jewelry brand, ask whether the manufacturer can work from technical drawings, reference photographs or physical samples.

Discuss material selection, design feasibility, prototyping, logo requirements and packaging before approving production.

Learn more about QIUHE OEM/ODM jewelry manufacturing.

5. Confirm Sampling and Quality Control Procedures

Product samples allow buyers to evaluate dimensions, finishing, appearance, comfort and workmanship. Confirm which specifications will be used to approve samples and inspect bulk production.

A practical quality plan should address incoming materials, production checks, final inspection, defect criteria and corrective actions.

6. Understand Market Compliance Requirements

Jewelry requirements vary by destination market, product type and materials. Buyers should identify applicable chemical restrictions, nickel release requirements, labeling obligations and testing documentation before placing orders.

For EU and UK markets, relevant REACH-related requirements may need to be considered. US requirements may vary by federal and state rules and by the intended customer group.

Ask suppliers what documentation is available and whether additional product-specific testing will be necessary.

7. Check Production Lead Times and Shipping Terms

Confirm the estimated sampling schedule, production lead time, packaging requirements and shipping arrangements before confirming a purchase order.

Clarify the agreed Incoterms, delivery responsibilities and communication procedures for production delays.

8. Start With a Trial Order

A trial order can help evaluate product consistency, packaging, documentation, communication and delivery performance before committing to larger volumes.

Keep written records of approved samples, specifications, order quantities, quality requirements and delivery terms.
