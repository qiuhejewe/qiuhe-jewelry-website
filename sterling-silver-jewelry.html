Why Choose QIUHE as Your 925 Sterling Silver Jewelry Manufacturer?

QIUHE Fashion Jewelry Ltd is a direct jewelry manufacturer based in Dongguan, China. We manufacture 925 sterling silver jewelry in-house for wholesale buyers, jewelry brands and retailers.
