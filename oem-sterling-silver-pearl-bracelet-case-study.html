Customer-Designed 925 Sterling Silver Freshwater Pearl Bracelet

A completed OEM jewelry manufacturing project based on a customer's original design, produced by QIUHE Jewelry Company in Dongguan, China.

Project Overview

The customer provided the original bracelet design. QIUHE Jewelry Company was responsible for manufacturing the finished jewelry according to the supplied design.

This completed order demonstrates our experience in OEM production of customer-designed sterling silver jewelry.

Product Specifications

Product: Freshwater pearl bracelet

Manufacturing Type: OEM — customer-provided design

Metal: 925 sterling silver

Pearls: Freshwater pearls

Decorative Elements: Zirconia-set beads

Closure: Lobster clasp

Additional Feature: Adjustable extension chain

Project Status: Completed order

Manufacturing Location: Dongguan, Guangdong, China

From Customer Design to Finished Bracelet

The finished bracelet features an alternating arrangement of freshwater pearls and sparkling zirconia-set beads. A lobster clasp and extension chain provide adjustable fastening, with a pearl accent at the chain end.

The product photographs show the completed bracelet, fastening details, and appearance when worn. The design belongs to the customer and is displayed with permission.

OEM Jewelry Manufacturing Capabilities

QIUHE Jewelry Company supports jewelry brands, retailers, and wholesale buyers seeking manufacturing services based on their own designs, drawings, photographs, or samples.

Our broader product categories include sterling silver jewelry, stainless steel jewelry, bracelets, necklaces, earrings, and rings.

Quality Control Approach

QIUHE's general manufacturing quality control procedures include material inspection, in-process inspection, visual inspection, dimension inspection, plating and finish inspection, and final inspection.

These procedures describe our general manufacturing approach and are not presented as independent third-party certification of this order.
